`ocr-layout.pdf` is the synthetic two-page scanned agreement generated by
`browser-smoke.mjs`. Its four intended headings are Definitions, Payment,
Termination and Governing Law; those labels are specified independently of parser
output.

`ocr-layout.json` freezes actual browser OCR lines and Accurate/Fast model detections
from the failing run. It deliberately retains the spurious OCR `o` fragment under
Governing Law. The replay compares native and WASM final graphs and requires the
four intended headings without that fragment. It needs neither model inference
nor a server.

The Fast capture leaves eight lines uncovered. Its replay must match upstream
rejection of the incomplete layout, without publishing raw heading labels.
